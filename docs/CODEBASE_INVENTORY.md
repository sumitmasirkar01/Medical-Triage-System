## Legacy

Everything under `legacy/` is excluded from the supported application. The files
were relocated rather than deleted so their history and potentially useful ideas
remain available during migration. This includes the former `core/` prototype,
its unverified checkpoints, and the superseded sample-DICOM generator. Supported
fixtures are generated by `scripts/create_demo_dicom.py`.

## Generated and temporary files

`docs/generated/` and `docs/.qa_rsna_report/` contain reproducible render/QA
outputs and are ignored by version control. Python and pytest caches are also
ignored.

## Promotion rule

A component becomes supported only when it has a documented input/output contract,
checkpoint and preprocessing provenance where applicable, deterministic local
tests, and claims traceable to saved evidence.
